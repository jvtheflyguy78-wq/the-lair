Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The Lair is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what information it handles and where that information goes.

What stays on your computer

Your account, conversations, files, projects, settings and any keys you add are stored in The Lair's folder on your own computer. They are not sent to the people who make or share The Lair, and we have no server that collects them. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and saved keys are encrypted.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with The Lair on your computer, so it can create or open your account. The Lair asks only for this basic profile information (the openid, email and profile permissions). It does not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data unless you separately connect one of those services in The Lair, and that information also stays on your computer. Google's use of your information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Services you connect

When you connect an AI provider or another service, The Lair sends that service the content needed for what you ask it to do, such as your messages to an AI model. Those services handle that content under their own privacy policies. Features that read the web contact the websites involved.

No tracking

The Lair has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Removing your information

You can delete your account in Settings, or uninstall The Lair, which removes its folder and everything in it from your computer. You can also remove The Lair's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
