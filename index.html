The Lair is a desktop app for Windows that brings chat, research, coding, video and automations together in one place. It runs on your computer, and your work stays there.

Getting it

  1. Get the installer from the person who shared The Lair with you.
  2. Unzip it and double-click "Install The Lair".
  3. Open The Lair from your desktop and create an account.
